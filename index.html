ZYRON STUDIOS · COMPLETE UPDATE HISTORY

TAG! Update Log · v4–v17

Milestones from the original playable realms through the current V17 release. Older entries are historical highlights, not exhaustive patch-by-patch records.

V17 · SOLID STRUCTURES & IN-MATCH FRIENDS

Fixed custom structure geometry so collision boxes line up with structures drawn in the level editor. Stationary and moving structures are solid from the top, bottom, left and right: jumping into their underside stops the jump and sends the runner back down. The in-match friend mailbox also supports manual refresh and automatic refresh on Nakama notifications, with outgoing, incoming, decline, resend, accept and mutual friendship records verified.

V16 · MOBILE PLAY, SOLID ARENAS & FRIEND MAILBOX

Responsive phone and tablet layouts, dynamic touch controls for each human player, tighter round-start spawns, a shorter arena view, solid structure collision (the green drink still grants temporary noclip), and a friend-request mailbox with accept and decline actions.

V15 · SMART BOTS & ARENA ARCHITECTURE

Rebuilt bot movement around platform-to-platform route planning, smarter target prediction, evasive flanks, active patrols, tactical jumps and varied dashes. Arena platforms are now automatically repositioned to avoid overlapping structures, and decorative architecture uses distinct, spaced positions in each realm.

V14 · RUNNER IDENTITY & PROFILE

Added player-name editing directly from Profile with unique-name enforcement; empty profiles stay nameless until a name is chosen; fixed Profile navigation to the title and leaderboard; custom profile pictures can be uploaded from your computer; added the Changed and Shiny achievement for uploading a custom picture.

V4 · REALMS IN MOTION

Original TAG! foundation, local chase gameplay and the first three arenas: Emberglass Foundry, Astral Archive and Drowned Observatory.

V5 · THE REALM EXPANSION

Expanded the world roster with Lunar Frontier, Greenland Trail, Neon City Rush, Overgrown Mall, Shadow Ninja Dojo, Sunscorch Canyon, Frostfall Peak, Cloudline Skyport and Ashen Caldera.

V6 · READY ROOM & ROUND FLOW

Added a player ready room, player count setup, bot support foundations, pre-round countdown and more defined round flow.

V7 · MORE WAYS TO PLAY

Added coins, orbs, jump pads, potions, dash gameplay, expanded language support, cinematic results and the Argentine secret arenas La Bombonera and El Monumental.

V8 · CAMERA & COMPETITION

Introduced adaptive camera framing, off-screen indicators, arena boundaries, tighter platform routes, FOV settings, tournament brackets, Infection Tag and Hot Potato.

V9 · GAMEPLAY & POLISH

Refined bot movement, tournament progression, menu and settings flow, player presentation, map behavior and gameplay systems based on earlier builds.

V10 · OFFICIAL FIRST BETA

Consolidated the first friend-playtest beta with expanded menu navigation, local progress, achievements, map roster, tournament options and the accumulated TAG! feature set.

V11 · HEAVEN, HELL & CREATOR

Added Heaven and Hell arenas, cinematic countdowns, up to three modifiers, number-key abilities, custom round lengths, FOV changes, Munci movement updates and the first local Block Level Creator.

V13 · RUNNERS, REWARDS & STADIUM FIXES

Fixed red-player coin pickups; every player can equip a separate accessory; rebuilt accessory models; added Angry Muncer, Champion and Creator achievements; refreshed La Bombonera in Boca blue and gold and El Monumental in River red and white.

V12 · SOUND, COINS & CREATOR POLISH

Rebuilt reliable per-map procedural music and custom music playback handling, made coin pickups larger and easier to collect, added quick settings to Pause, fixed custom round seconds, upgraded the What's New history, and expanded the level editor with decorations and recoloring for every object.
